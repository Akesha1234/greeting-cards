## 🎨 Brand Design Guidelines
- **Midnight Plum** (`#27232D`): Dark vintage background & typewriter lettering
- **Cream** (`#F8E8D8`): Natural cardstock paper texture
- **Sassy Coral** (`#E85D4A`): Accent titles & borders
- **Acid Lime** (`#D8E36A`): Interactive action buttons & wax seal
- **Typography**: Google Fonts *Special Elite* (Vintage Typewriter) & *Montserrat*
- **Page Turning**: StPageFlip with natural Bézier corner curling and cardstock shadows (inspired by American Greetings & FlippingBook)
- **Signature Wording**: Strictly `"Sent with best wishes FROM [Sender]"`

## 🛠️ Personalization Workflow (Dropdown 3 Choices)
Every card includes a built-in personalization modal with the 3 choices requested by the client:
1. **MY OWN CAPTION (Suggested)**: Displays the store's suggested humorous/witty message.
2. **CUSTOM (Write Your Own)**: Allows customer to type any personal message.
3. **BLANK (Empty Inside)**: Delivers an empty cardstock interior.
- **Dynamic Signature**: Always formatted as `"Sent with best wishes FROM [Sender]"`.
- **Live Preview Before Sending**: Customers can test the full unboxing and page-turn animation with their personalized message before finalizing.
